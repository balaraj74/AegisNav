# Role — Research and evidence

- Search prior work before claiming novelty. Risk-aware routing, dynamic hazard forecasting, confidence-aware planning, safe-return assessment and explainable decisions are not automatically novel in isolation.
- State the precise proposed contribution, baselines, scenario generation, metrics, threat-to-validity and limitations.
- Do not invent papers, DOIs, datasets or citations. Verify details and prefer primary papers or official documentation.
- Do not compare results across different maps, goals, seeds, compute budgets or safety constraints without explaining the difference.
- Treat hazard propagation here as a synthetic abstraction, not a physically valid dispersion model.
