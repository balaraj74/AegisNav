## Vendored Gazebo Fuel models requiring attribution (CC BY 4.0, added 2026-10-30)

These models are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Credit them wherever the work is shown or redistributed, including the demo video credits. The attribution lines below are
recorded by `tools/fetch_fuel_assets.py` in `tools/fuel_assets.lock.json`.

| Fuel model | Version | Local directory | Attribution | Archive SHA-256 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fog Emitter | 5 | `models/fog_emitter` | "Fog Emitter" v5 by Carlos Aguero (OpenRobotics), https://app.gazebosim.org/OpenRobotics/fuel/models/Fog%20Emitter/5, CC BY 4.0 | `70cec7732161…` |

The model is unmodified on disk. Its `fog.png` texture is referenced, unmodified, by the hazard fire/smoke/gas particle effects
(`aegisnav_ros/hazard_tiles.py`); the colour ramps those effects use are generated by `tools/generate_world_assets.py`
(`models/aegisnav_effects`).

Known gap: the `Fire truck` model references textures that are not in its archive, so it renders untextured.
Collision geometry from these models is not used for planning; the planning/occupancy grid comes from
`aegisnav_core.scenarios`. Interior props and victims sit on cells that grid marks as blocked; the collapsed
buildings and vehicles stand outside the walled arena.

All other meshes and textures in the repository (walls, floor tiles, hazard tiles, source effects and their colour ramps) are
generated from primitives by `tools/generate_world_assets.py` / `tools/world_rescue_building.py`, or reuse the textures above.
No HDRIs, videos or datasets are included.
